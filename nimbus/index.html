Nimbus

A light, private, multi-profile browser for Android.

Nimbus lets you keep separate browsing profiles in one app — one for work, one for social, one for shopping — each with its own cookies, logins and settings, so accounts never clash. It blocks ads and trackers, includes a built-in download engine, a password vault that never leaves your phone, and optional sync through your own Google Drive.

Download Nimbus for Android

Highlights

  • Unlimited isolated profiles — every profile looks like its own device
  • Ad & tracker blocking with an auto-updating block list
  • Built-in download engine with pause, resume and parallel segments
  • Password vault encrypted on your device — never synced, never uploaded
  • Optional Google Drive sync of profiles and bookmarks (your Drive, your data)
  • User scripts, themes, QR scanner and more
