Nimbus Privacy Policy

Effective: 12 November 2026

The short version

Nimbus is a web browser. Your browsing data lives on your phone. We run no analytics, show no ads of our own, and never receive, sell or share your personal data.

Data stored on your device

Profiles, bookmarks, history, tabs, downloads and settings are stored locally on your device so the browser can work. You can clear them at any time from Settings → Browsing Data, or by clearing the app's data in Android settings.

Password vault

Passwords you choose to save are encrypted on your device with the Android Keystore and protected by your PIN or fingerprint. They are never transmitted anywhere, and they are excluded from backups and from Drive sync.

Optional Google Drive sync

If you turn on Drive Sync, Nimbus stores one sync file in the private app folder (appDataFolder) of your own Google Drive account. It contains your profiles, bookmarks, settings and user scripts — never passwords. Only you can access your Drive. The developer cannot see this file or any of your Drive data. You can disconnect sync at any time in Settings, and deleting the app removes its access.

Websites you visit

Pages you open are loaded directly between your device and those websites, under each website's own privacy policy. Nimbus blocks known ad and tracker domains on your device; blocking happens locally.

Permissions

  • Internet — to load web pages and downloads
  • Camera — only for scanning QR codes, when you open the scanner
  • Notifications — download progress and update notices
  • Biometric / PIN — to unlock the app and the password vault
  • Install packages — to install Nimbus updates you approve

Children

Nimbus is a general-purpose browser and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
